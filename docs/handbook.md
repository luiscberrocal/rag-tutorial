# Byte Bistro Employee Handbook

## Opening hours

Byte Bistro opens at 7:00 AM Monday through Friday and at 8:00 AM on Saturdays and Sundays. We close at 6:00 PM every day. The cafe is closed on Christmas Day, New Year's Day and Thanksgiving.

## Wi-Fi

The guest Wi-Fi network is "ByteBistro-Guest" and the password is "latte4ever". The staff network is "ByteBistro-Staff" and its password is only given out by the manager. Never share the staff password with customers.

## Dog policy

Dogs are welcome on the patio only. Please keep a water bowl filled by the patio door. Dogs are not allowed inside the cafe, with the exception of service animals, which are always welcome.

## Alarm code

The front door alarm code is 417. Enter it within 30 seconds of opening the door in the morning, and set it again as the last step of closing. If the alarm goes off by mistake, call the monitoring company at 555-0142 and give the password "croissant".

## Espresso machine

Purge the steam wand after every drink and backflush the group heads with cleaner at the end of every day. If the pressure gauge drops below 8 bar, stop using the machine and tell the shift lead.

## Closing checklist

Wipe down all tables, stack the patio chairs, empty the pastry case into the day-old bin, mop the floor behind the counter, count the register, and set the alarm before locking the front door.

## Breaks

Shifts longer than 6 hours include one paid 30-minute break. Shifts of 4 to 6 hours include one paid 15-minute break. Staff drinks are free during your shift, and pastries are 50% off.

## Dress code

Wear a black or grey top, closed-toe non-slip shoes, and the Byte Bistro apron. Long hair must be tied back. Name tags are required on every shift.

## Payments

We accept cash, all major credit cards, and mobile payments. We do not accept checks. Refunds over $20 must be approved by the shift lead.

## Allergens

Our almond and oat milks are stored on the left side of the fridge. Always use a clean pitcher for non-dairy milks, and tell customers that our pastries are baked in a kitchen that handles nuts, gluten, and eggs.

## Calling in sick

If you cannot make your shift, text the manager, Dana, at 555-0199 at least 2 hours before your shift starts. You are responsible for posting the open shift in the staff group chat.
